### 2. Field: "Tell us about the project's reach and impact"
*(Copy & paste the text below)*

```text
BTMap (https://github.com/JUreK646/btmapv2) is Poland's primary open-source telecommunications intelligence and civic RF infrastructure mapping platform.

Unlike traditional utility libraries distributed via package managers, BTMap is an open-data community web application and data pipeline. It aggregates, standardizes, and visualizes official telecommunications regulatory registries from the Polish Office of Electronic Communications (UKE) and public electromagnetic field (EMF/PEM) environmental reports from the National Institute of Telecommunications (SI2PEM).

Project Impact & Community Reach:
1. Public Infrastructure Transparency: BTMap serves thousands of telecommunications engineers, RF planners, field technicians, and everyday citizens across Poland tracking 5G C-band (n78), LTE-A, GSM-R, and microwave radio relay networks.
2. Civic Open Data & Environmental Safety: We parse and expose real-world accredited laboratory radiation measurements, giving the public open and transparent access to local electromagnetic emission reports that are otherwise locked inside government PDF filings.
3. Open Source Health & Standards: The project strictly adheres to open standards, featuring a full OpenSSF Scorecard setup, GitHub CodeQL security analysis, Dependabot supply chain audits, structured community contribution pipelines, and an MIT license.
```
